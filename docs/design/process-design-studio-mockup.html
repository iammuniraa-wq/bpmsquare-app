What the ratings mean

Standard Works out of the box; you only load your data.

Needs setup Standard, once an admin sets it up in Settings.

Partly covered Most is there; a piece needs a change we list in the next step.

Not covered Not in BPMSquare today. We say what we would build, or what to keep elsewhere.

A rating is only given with a named module, screen or setting behind it. If we are not sure, the line says “check with us” instead of guessing.

What the effort bands mean

Small — a setting or your own fields; hours, done during setup. Medium — a change only for your workspace, or an existing connector; days. Large — something new in the product or a new integration; weeks, discussed on the call.

Bands follow the kind of change; they are not guessed. Indicative, never a price.
