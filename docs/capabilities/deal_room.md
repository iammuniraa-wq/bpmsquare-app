# deal_room
status: experimental
plain: The Deal Room. A private room for one tender deal where your own colleagues discuss the bid in one place instead of by email: messages with links and files, questions with an owner and a due date, and pinned decisions. Only people in your workspace can see it.
rules:
- It exists only inside GCC tender deals and needs that module; it appears as a Room tab on a tender deal, and as a full page on a phone.
- Only workspace members who can open the Pipeline can read or post; no client, guest or outside person can reach it.
- The deal's owner and team are added automatically and others by name; members see an unread count and get an email summary at most every 15 minutes.
- A message is plain text and cannot be edited; removing it keeps a record that it was removed, and its text is no longer shown.
- A question stays open until someone marks it answered, and the Tender tab shows how many are open.
- Files are limited to documents, drawings and pictures, up to 5 per message and 15 MB each, and open through short-lived private links.
- The flag is set by a platform admin; a workspace without it sees nothing new.
