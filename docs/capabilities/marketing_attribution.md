# marketing_attribution
status: experimental
plain: See which campaigns, newsletters, forms, journeys, WhatsApp messages and events started or touched your deals and revenue, by first touch, last touch or an even split, with a plain note on what that can and cannot tell you.
rules:
- It only reads: it never changes a deal, a quote, an invoice or a person.
- A touch is something a customer did or was sent (a click, a form, a registration, a message); each is taken from the activity already recorded and is never counted twice.
- Started a deal means touches before the deal was created, shared by the model you choose; touched a deal means any touch in the 90 days before it closed or today, so touched figures overlap and must not be added up.
- Revenue is the deal amount for won deals and the paid amount on its invoices; it is a guide to where to look, not proof that a campaign caused a sale.
- The figures are recomputed every few hours and show when they were last updated.
- Switched on per workspace by a platform admin and needs Marketing campaigns; until then there is no menu item and no work is done.
