# marketing_scoring
status: experimental
plain: Scores each lead from what the person actually did, moves it through clear stages, and checks consent before anyone is contacted. Staff can record what a person told them, and bring a sheet of opt-ins in.
rules:
- Consent is required by default; an admin can relax it for a country, but not for India.
- A score always shows the reasons behind it.
- A person in sales can record that someone agreed or withdrew, with a required note on how it was obtained; answers are only ever added, never edited.
- A sheet of opt-ins can be imported in the Data Workbench: each row needs its basis, is matched to an existing contact by email, and keeps the file name as evidence. It never creates contacts.
- Switched on per workspace by a platform admin; until then no screen or lead changes.
