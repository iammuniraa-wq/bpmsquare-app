# marketing_events
status: experimental
plain: Plan a webinar, show, roadshow or site visit, let people register through a form, check them in on the day, see how many came, and start a follow-up journey for those who came or those who did not.
rules:
- Registration happens through an existing form, so the person's consent is recorded exactly as the form says; an event never records consent by itself.
- A person registers once per event; a second submission changes nothing and is not counted twice.
- Staff check people in; an attended person stays attended, a no-show can still be marked attended, and a cancelled person can be registered again.
- Registering, attending and not coming are recorded as activity so journeys and revenue reports can see them.
- The follow-up only starts a journey you already built and turned on, once per person; it never sends anything by itself and the journey keeps every consent and quiet-hours rule.
- Switched on per workspace by a platform admin and needs Marketing campaigns and Forms; until then there is no menu item and no form is linked to an event.
