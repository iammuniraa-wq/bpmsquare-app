# site_receipts
status: experimental
plain: A person on site confirms on a phone what physically arrived: pick the delivery, count the items, record short, damaged or rejected pieces with a reason and photos, and confirm. A receipt is evidence and creates no cost.
rules:
- A receipt takes three steps on a phone: pick the delivery (a buy-out package or something else), check the items starting from All OK, then confirm with the receiver's name, the driver and vehicle, an optional signature and photos.
- Accepted plus rejected equals received; a reason is required when an item is short, over, rejected or not in good condition, and more than expected is flagged and never blocked.
- A confirmed receipt cannot be changed; a manager cancels it with a reason and the site person enters it again, and both are logged.
- A second truck for the same delivery note is a second receipt, and the note shows the balance.
- Any shortfall, rejection or damage tells the project manager by e-mail and in the Nova inbox.
- Each buy-out package shows what was awarded, the value received on site (accepted quantity times the awarded rate) and what is still to receive; it is named Received on site and never progress or installed.
- A receipt works with or without the element master: the element code and building are plain text until that master exists.
- Switched on per workspace by a platform admin and needs Contract projects; until then there is no menu item, route, tab or button.
