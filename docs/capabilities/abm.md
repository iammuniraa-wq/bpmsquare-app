# abm
status: experimental
plain: Choose the companies that matter, work each one as a team towards a stated goal, and see how many were reached, engaged, in a deal and won, counted by company instead of by lead.
rules:
- A company can be in several programs at once, each with its own tier, progress, owner and next step.
- Progress is a status set by fixed rules from what really happened (a quote opened, a deal opened), and a person can override it; an override always needs a reason and shows who made it.
- Buying stage is read from Buying signals and is never changed or re-scored here; there is no ABM score.
- The target list is a snapshot taken from a target group or segment and never changes by itself.
- A buying group names people and their role for one program; naming someone sends nothing and needs no consent.
- Switched on per workspace by a platform admin and needs Marketing campaigns; until then there is no menu item and no card on any other screen.
