# journeys
status: experimental
plain: Automated follow-ups that start from something that happened, such as a quote being sent, wait a few days, send a newsletter version and create a task for the owner.
rules:
- Every message checks the person's consent at the moment it is sent; no consent, no message, and the run log says why.
- A person goes through a journey once; nobody is entered twice.
- Messages wait for quiet hours and the weekly day off, and a person gets at most one journey email a day.
- Promotional steps pause while the company has an open case that has dragged on or an overdue invoice.
- A demo workspace never reaches a real customer; the email output setting applies exactly as for campaigns.
- Turning a journey on that would enter more than 200 people asks for confirmation first.
- Try it on one person shows the path and the reasons and sends nothing.
- Switched on per workspace by a platform admin and needs Marketing campaigns; until then there is no menu item and no runner work.
