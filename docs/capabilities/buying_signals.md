# buying_signals
status: experimental
plain: Shows which of your accounts look ready to buy, in plain stages (Quiet, Looking, Comparing, Deciding), with every point of the score named and sourced, one next step per account, and a before-and-after view of which accounts a campaign moved.
rules:
- The score is a fixed, explainable rubric built only from your own records: quotes, deals, cases, contracts, campaign and newsletter clicks. It is never guessed by AI and is the same every time you open it.
- Older signals count for less and drop out after 60 days; no single source can push an account past its cap.
- Stage and score can be used as audience fields in Segmentation, so a campaign can target accounts that are Comparing.
- An account owner is told in the Nova inbox when an account moves up to Comparing or Deciding.
- Switched on per workspace by a platform admin and needs Marketing campaigns; until then there is no menu item and nothing is calculated.
