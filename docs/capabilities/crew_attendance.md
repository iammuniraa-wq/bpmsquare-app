# crew_attendance
status: experimental
plain: Attendance Anomalies. It reviews aliased attendance facts after the day closes and tells supervisors about patterns a daily list hides: a site, a day or a setting, rather than just a person.
rules:
- It needs AI automation and Workforce, and stays off for a real workspace until the owner has the customer's written acceptance of the processing.
- The model never sees a name, code, phone, e-mail, photo or coordinates: each person is a per-run alias, and names are put back only in the browser of a person allowed to see them.
- Findings are review suggestions: they carry what was observed, what else could explain it and what the supervisor could do, and never judge honesty or suggest a pay or disciplinary step.
- A supervisor sees findings for their own sites only and never findings about themselves; employees see nothing.
- The only action it can propose is a recheck request that the supervisor edits and sends as their own message.
