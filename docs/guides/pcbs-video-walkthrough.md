# PCBS video: Estimator walkthrough with voice-over + BPMSquare overview

A plan you can follow even if you have never made a video. About **7 minutes**.
Record the screen in short scenes, read the script, join the scenes. No
special skills needed.

## 1. What this video must do

- Show PCBS, in plain words, how an enquiry email becomes a priced precast
  quote in a few minutes.
- Give a short, honest picture of BPMSquare as a whole.
- Make them want the next conversation, not answer every question.

**Say it is an early version.** The estimator works end to end and you have
more planned. That is true and builds trust.

**Do not promise** (not built or not tested with them yet): pushing data live
into their Dynamics 365 (the connector is built; "we will test it together on
your system"), reading drawings or Tekla/Revit files, an AI-made image of the
building, or any exact price (the numbers are illustrative placeholders).

## 2. Before you record (15 minutes, do this once)

1. Use the **demo workspace** (`app.bpmsquare.com`), in a clean browser window
   (Chrome or Edge), signed in as the demo admin.
2. Make sure **Estimates** is in the menu (the estimator switch is on for the
   demo) and the demo catalogue SQL has run (precast products and kits).
3. **Delete test data** from earlier tries: any test account such as "Al-Rashid
   Trading" and its quote, so the take starts clean and "create account" is a
   real moment. Keep the "Sample villa, Riyadh (illustrative)" estimate: you
   will show it at the end.
4. Screen: set the display to **1920×1080**, browser zoom **110 percent**
   (Ctrl and +), close every other tab, **hide bookmarks**, turn on Do Not
   Disturb (Windows: Focus assist) so no notification pops up on screen.
5. Open this file on a **second screen or your phone**, or print the script.
6. Have the sample email ready to paste (see section 5).
7. Quiet room, phone on silent. A headset microphone beats the laptop one.

## 3. Tools (free, already on Windows)

- **Recording:** `Windows + Alt + R` (Xbox Game Bar) records the browser
  window, or use **Clipchamp** (Start menu) → Record → Screen. Pick "this
  window", and tick microphone if you want to speak while recording.
- **Editing:** Clipchamp: drag the scenes onto the timeline, trim the starts
  and ends, add a title card, auto-captions (one click), export **1080p MP4**.
- **Two ways to do voice:**
  1. *Easiest:* record screen and voice together, scene by scene. If you
     stumble, stop and redo only that scene (each is 20 to 40 seconds).
  2. *Cleaner:* record the screen silently while you do the clicks, then
     record the voice in Clipchamp (Record → Audio) while watching the video.
- Do **not** try to do one perfect 7-minute take. Scenes are easier.

## 4. How to speak

- Slow down by about a quarter. Short sentences. Pause between ideas.
- Smile slightly when you speak, it changes your voice.
- Say a number or a name, then stop (let the screen carry it).
- A small mistake is fine. If you lose the thread, stop, breathe, redo the
  scene.
- Move the mouse slowly, and rest it while you talk. Never wave it around.

## 5. The scenes and script (read the text in quotes)

**Scene 1: Title (10 s).** Title card: "BPMSquare Project Estimator, for PCBS".
> "Hello, this is a short walkthrough of how BPMSquare turns a customer's
> enquiry into a priced precast quote."

**Scene 2: BPMSquare in 60 seconds (60 s).** Show the dashboard, then click
through the menu slowly: Accounts, Standard Quotes, Products, RFQ, Marketing.
> "BPMSquare is one platform for the whole customer journey. Your accounts and
> contacts; quotes and pricing; products with their bills of materials; supplier
> requests for quotation; and marketing and follow-up. It is built for companies
> that sell projects, in English and Arabic, and it connects to your ERP,
> including Dynamics 365. Everything you see starts from one customer record,
> so sales, pricing and marketing never work from different lists."

**Scene 3: Start with the customer (30 s).** Menu → **Estimates** → **New
estimate**. Point at the empty page, the **Customer** card at the top.
> "A new estimate starts blank, with the customer first. The villa and the
> price appear only once there is something to price."

**Scene 4: Paste the email (40 s).** Paste the sample email below into the
email box, click **Fill in from the email**.
> "Your sales team gets an email from a customer. They paste it here. BPMSquare
> reads it and fills in the details: two floors, around 220 square metres per
> floor, premium finish, stairs, a boundary wall. It also picks up who sent it."

**Scene 5: Create the customer (30 s).** The **From this email** card appears.
Click **Create the account and the contact**.
> "The sender is not in the system yet. One click creates the account and the
> contact, with the email and phone from the signature. Nothing is created until
> you press the button."

**Scene 6: The villa and the price (50 s).** Scroll to the 3D villa and the
price. Rotate the villa slowly. Change **External finish** from Premium to
Basic and back.
> "Here is the building, made from the numbers, with the actual precast pieces:
> walls, slabs, columns, stairs. Change the finish and the price updates
> straight away. This is not a picture, the bill of materials is added up from
> these pieces."

**Scene 7: How the price is built (50 s).** Open **How the price is built**,
then **Build schedule and risk**.
> "Your team can open how the price is built: materials, production, transport,
> erection and overhead, then margin and VAT. The schedule section shows how
> long casting takes with your plant capacity, and a risk range for the cost.
> These rates are examples. In the project we set them with your numbers."

**Scene 8: Products and kits (40 s).** Open **Bill of materials**.
> "The lines come from your product catalogue. A wall panel kit is made of
> concrete, rebar, insulation and connectors, and its cost is rolled up from
> those parts. Change a material cost once, and every estimate follows."

**Scene 9: Create the quote (40 s).** Click **Create quote**, then open the
quote. Show it is a normal customer quote.
> "One click turns the estimate into a customer quote, grouped in sections. The
> customer sees prices, not your internal costs."

**Scene 10: Send to your ERP (30 s).** Back to the estimate, open **Send to
your ERP**, show the download options.
> "For your ERP, you can export the item list and the work breakdown as files.
> The Dynamics 365 connection is built, and we would test it together on your
> environment before anything goes live."

**Scene 11: Close (30 s).** Show the estimate list, then the menu once more.
> "This is an early version. Everything shown can be shaped with your real
> products, rates and rules. We would be glad to do that with you. Thank you."

Total about 7 minutes.

## 6. The sample email to paste

```
Subject: Precast villa quotation - Dammam

Dear Sir,

I plan to build a two-storey villa in Dammam and would like a precast quotation.
Each floor is about 11 m by 20 m, so around 220 m2 per floor.
Wall height is 3.2 m floor to floor, and the external walls are 20 cm thick.
We would like a premium external finish, precast stairs with two flights,
and a boundary wall of 80 m at 2.4 m high.

Please send your price and the delivery time.

Regards,
Khalid Al-Rashid
Al-Rashid Trading Co.
+966 50 123 4567
khalid@alrashid.example
```

Do a **dry run once** first, off camera, to see exactly what appears, then
delete the test account.

## 7. After recording

1. Trim the start and end of each scene, remove long silences.
2. Add the title card and one line at the end with your name and contact.
3. Turn on **auto-captions**, read them quickly for wrong words (PCBS terms).
4. Export **1080p MP4**. Check it plays with sound on your phone.
5. Share as a private link (OneDrive or Google Drive "anyone with the link"),
   not as a big email attachment.

## 8. Optional upgrades (only if you have time)

- An Arabic version of the captions (same video, translated text).
- A 90-second silent teaser cut (scenes 3 to 6 only) for WhatsApp.
- Scene 8 can show the RFQ button on a quote line if they ask about supplier
  prices.

## 9. If something looks wrong while you record

Do not fix it on camera. Note the page and what you saw, finish the scene,
and send it to us. Small rough edges are fine in an early version, but never
show an error message: redo the scene.
