The daily flow

1Email arrives
Inbox shows it. Create from this email → Deal makes the tender (you choose what an email becomes).
2Bid decision
Propose bid decision (Go or No-go, with a reason). An approver approves; the tender moves to Estimating.
3Bill of quantities
Bill of quantities → Upload BOQ (.xlsx). Columns are read for you; check, then Create the deal lines.
4Cost it
Costing → New RFQ: pick suppliers, Send; replies come by email or via Enter reply. Or New estimate: pick a template, fill the inputs, and the cost works out from the template’s rates.
5Compare and award
Costing → Compare and award. Lowest valid price is marked; awarding another needs a reason. Cost + markup becomes the rate.
6Submit the bid
Bid tab lists what is missing (unpriced lines, old prices, no approval). Submit saves version 1 and its total.
7Award
Set stage Awarded → Won → Create contract project. Contract value, budget and planned margin come across.
8Run the project
Budget, buy-out, variations, invoicing and handover live on the contract project.

Set up once (Settings)

SettingWhat to do
Connectors → Gmail or Mail serverConnect the tenders mailbox, then Test connection. Gmail needs an App Password (Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords); your normal password is refused. A company mailbox uses Mail server with its SMTP and IMAP details.
Inbox channelsNew channel on that mailbox. Fill Only from these senders and/or Subject contains (for example tender, RFQ, BOQ) so only tender mail is copied in. Leave Team empty.
RFQ → Mail channelChoose Your own mailbox to send RFQs from your address and read replies back.
TendersPick the approvers. Keep “Allow self-approval” off in daily use.
Email outputSend to business partners for real use. “Specified address” redirects every email to one inbox: for testing only.
Estimate templatesSettings → Estimate templates holds the inputs, rates and rules an estimate is worked out from. Add the starters, then replace their placeholder rates with your own. Editing a template never re-prices an existing estimate.
SuppliersGive each supplier an email address. A supplier with none cannot be sent an RFQ.
PeopleCreate a login from each employee (Employees → Create login). Everyone sets their own password at first sign-in.

Good to know

  • Use a dedicated mailbox (tenders@…), not a personal inbox. A channel reads the whole mailbox; the filter limits what is kept.
  • Mail from the mailbox’s own address is ignored on purpose. Test from a different address.
  • The mailbox is read every 5 minutes. Read mail now on the channel does it at once.
  • An RFQ must be sent (not Draft) before Compare and award appears on the tender.
  • The BOQ is uploaded again from the Bill of quantities tab, even if it came as an email attachment.
  • The bid end date is not read from the email text: set it on the tender.
  • Prices older than the validity window (Tenders settings) are flagged stale; reprice or ask again.
  • Platform emails (invites, approval notices) need the email key on the server: already set for KAMCO.

If something does not work

  • Test connection fails: read the message. “Application-specific password required” means a Google App Password is needed.
  • Channel shows an error: open it; the line under Mailbox names the reason.
  • Email not in the Inbox: check the filter words and sender, then Read mail now.
